About

Editorial Profile

L’AVOIR — International Fashion Desk

Who is L’AVOIR?
L’AVOIR is an independent editorial publication from Argentina, developed with an international perspective across fashion, culture, industry and contemporary affairs.
Editorial Direction
L’AVOIR is independently directed and developed from Puerto Madryn, Patagonia, Argentina, with the aim of expanding its editorial reach without territorial limits.
Editorial Coverage
Fashion · Culture · Industry · International Affairs
International Fashion Desk
The International Fashion Desk is L’AVOIR’s specialist editorial unit dedicated to fashion houses, Fashion Weeks, creative industries, luxury, design, photography, cinema, sustainability and the business of fashion.
Markets Covered
Paris · Milan · London · New York · Madrid · Tokyo, with coverage expanding across international fashion and cultural markets.

Policies

Editorial Standards

L’AVOIR — Editorial Policies

Editorial Independence
L’AVOIR maintains editorial independence from advertisers, sponsors, public relations agencies and other external interests. Editorial decisions are made according to journalistic and cultural criteria.
Accuracy & Fact-Checking
L’AVOIR seeks to verify relevant information through reliable sources and available primary documentation before publication. When information is developing or incomplete, the publication identifies the uncertainty appropriately.
Corrections
When a material factual error is identified, L’AVOIR will correct the information as soon as reasonably possible. Corrections are made transparently and do not alter the editorial integrity of the original work.
Sponsored Content Disclosure
Paid partnerships, sponsored content and other commercial collaborations are identified clearly. Commercial relationships do not determine independent editorial coverage.
Image & Credit Policy
L’AVOIR aims to identify photographers, agencies, designers, houses and other rights holders whenever the relevant information is available. Images are used with appropriate attribution and in accordance with applicable rights and permissions.
Conflicts of Interest
L’AVOIR seeks to identify and manage potential conflicts of interest that could affect editorial judgment. Relevant commercial, personal or professional relationships are disclosed when they materially affect coverage.
Press & PR Policy
Press releases, invitations, samples and other materials received from brands or public relations representatives may inform coverage but do not guarantee publication, positive coverage or editorial endorsement.
